# ESCP-HC beyond the training range

Test data for the evaluation of changes beyond the training range (paper Table 2; supplement Sec. C.3, "Changes beyond the training range"). Generated by the ESCP-HC generator with the factor range widened from `u ~ U(-3, 3)` to `u ~ U(-4, 4)` (`--log-scale-limit 4`); namespace string, seeds and everything else are those of ESCP-HC. Only TEST and TEST_B (the paper's TE-B) are included; the generator also writes TRAIN and VAL files, which are not used.

Arrays: as in [escphc.md](escphc.md). All models are trained on ESCP-HC (L = 3). Of the 1,440 TEST + TEST_B queries, 916 have at least one factor outside the training range `[1.5 ** -3, 1.5 ** 3]` (tolerance 1e-6) in their context or in their 40 predicted steps; only these are evaluated. `datasets/labels.py` computes this flag (`beyond_range`).

Generation: `bash datasets/regenerate_all.sh OUT escphc_beyond`, i.e. `python -B -m escphc.cli collect --out OUT/escphc_beyond --log-scale-limit 4` (from `datasets/`). At the default `--log-scale-limit 3` the same command reproduces ESCP-HC.
