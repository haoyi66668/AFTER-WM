## ALFA sequences

`data/alfa/` contains sequences derived from the ALFA dataset:

> Azarakhsh Keipour, Mohammadreza Mousaei and Sebastian Scherer. ALFA: A Dataset for UAV Fault and Anomaly Detection. The International Journal of Robotics Research 40(2-3), 2021. https://doi.org/10.1177/0278364920966642. Data: https://doi.org/10.1184/R1/12707963.v1 (file processed.zip), licence CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

> Azarakhsh Keipour, Mohammadreza Mousaei and Sebastian Scherer. Automatic Real-time Anomaly Detection for Autonomous Aerial Vehicles. IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA), 2019. https://doi.org/10.1109/ICRA.2019.8794286.

Part of the ALFA recordings was published with the second paper, and the ALFA authors ask users of those recordings to cite it. Please cite both papers when you use the ALFA sequences.

The ALFA data are licensed under CC BY 4.0. The derived sequences in `data/alfa/` are distributed under the same licence (CC BY 4.0). No endorsement by the ALFA authors is implied.

Statement of changes (details in `datasets/cards/alfa.md`):

- the signals were read from the CSV files of the archive processed.zip and resampled to a 20 Hz grid (dt = 0.05 s), then stored as float32;
- the flights were cut into sequences of 301 states, one starting every 160 grid steps of a recording; a sequence is kept only if all used signals are present without long gaps and the aircraft is armed and in an autonomous mode;
- whole flights were assigned to the training, validation and test splits by stratified quotas with a fixed seed;
- failure labels and onset times were kept as analysis labels only.

The original ALFA files are not redistributed; `datasets/alfa/download_alfa.sh` downloads this archive from the data source above and checks its sha256.
